Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information emmettsutherland.com (“the site”) collects when you visit and how it is used. The site is run by Emmett Sutherland in Los Angeles, California.

Information collected

Visitor statistics. The site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they look at and how they found the site. Google Analytics collects:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay
  • the website or link that brought you here
  • your device type, browser, operating system and screen size
  • your approximate location (country, region and city), worked out from your IP address
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie, so repeat visits can be counted

Google Analytics does not store IP addresses, and I never see your IP address, your name or anything else that identifies you personally. I only see totals and trends.

Emails you send me. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you.

Embedded videos. Some pages show videos and video thumbnails from YouTube and Vimeo. Loading or playing them connects your browser to those services, which handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets two cookies on this site (named _ga and _ga_3ZWJXS002P) to tell visits apart. They last up to two years. The site uses no advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third parties

Information collected by Google Analytics is processed by Google LLC. Google may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites that use its services. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Google’s advertising features (Google signals and ad personalization) are turned off for this site. I do not sell or share your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the site does not load Google Analytics at all, so no statistics or cookies are collected for your visit. You can also opt out on any website with Google’s Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Reviewing your information

Visitor statistics aren’t linked to your name, so I can’t look them up or change them for an individual visitor. If you have emailed me and would like me to delete your messages, or if you have any questions about this policy, contact me at emmett@emmettsutherland.com.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date at the top.
